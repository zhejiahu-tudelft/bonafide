Reconciliation for Value-added Sales
The cost of gold, silver, platinum, palladium, copper, ruthenium, iridium, rhodium, rhenium, and osmium is passed through to customers and, therefore, the 
trends and comparisons of net sales are affected by movements in the market price of these metals. The use of value-added sales allows management to 
assess the impact of differences in net sales and analyze the resulting profitability without the distortion of metal pricing movements, which the Company 
believes would be useful information for investors.
